Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our handmade pipe cleaner bouquets.

Pipe cleaner flowers are handcrafted from high-density, velvety soft chenille stems over bendable wire cores. They are soft to touch, vibrant in color, completely pollen-free, and designed to stay in pristine shape indefinitely.

None at all! They don't need water, sunlight, or pruning. To keep them looking brand new, simply give them a gentle blow or light dusting once in a while. Keep them away from prolonged dampness.

You can add flowers to your shopping bag and click "Confirm Order via WhatsApp", or fill out our custom order form. Your order summary is formatted and sent directly to Dhanika on WhatsApp. We discuss availability, crafting schedule, and provide verified UPI payment details before shipping.

We deliver across all cities, towns, and PIN codes in Maharashtra including Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur, Nashik, Thane, Aurangabad, Kolhapur, and Solapur. Each bouquet is cushioned in sturdy packaging so it arrives in flawless condition.
