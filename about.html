Meet Dhanika

Crafting Forever Blooms, One Petal at a Time

Every gift should tell a story that outlasts the fleeting moment.

Like many flower lovers, Dhanika adored the breathtaking elegance of fresh bouquets, but felt a familiar sting of sadness seeing them wilt and end up in the dustbin within just three or four days.

Inspired by the timeless aesthetic of Korean and Japanese craft culture, Dhanika began experimenting with plush, premium-grade chenille wire stems (pipe cleaners). What started as a labor of love for family and friends blossomed into Fiona Florist by Dhanika.

Today, each rose, tulip, sunflower, and lily is lovingly hand-twisted, sculpted, wrapped, and bow-tied. These are not factory-molded plastic flowers — they are tactile, velvety keepsakes imbued with care, patience, and warmth.

How to Care for Your Bouquet

Your flowers are crafted to last indefinitely. Here are simple tips to keep them pristine:

🌬️

Gentle Dusting

Use a hairdryer on cool/low setting or a soft makeup brush to remove any dust gently.

💧

Keep Away from Water

Do not soak in water or expose to heavy rain, as internal wire cores should stay dry.

🖐️

Reshapeable Petals

Because chenille stems have bendable cores, you can adjust petal flairs and angles anytime!

☀️

Indoor Display

Looks gorgeous on work desks, vanity mirrors, bookshelves, or bedside stands.
